# Financial_Dashboard
This dashboard summarizes the financial performance data in financials dataset. It shows sales trends over time, sales by country and customer segment, and profit by product.
The Financial Performance dashboard presents a summary of sales and profitability using the financials data shown in the report. It brings together views of sales over time, sales across countries, sales by customer segment, and profit by product. These views help stakeholders review overall performance and compare which parts of the business contribute most to revenue and profit.

The report shows total sales of approximately $118.73 million, total profit of approximately $16.89 million, and an overall profit margin of about 14.23%. The Government segment is the largest contributor to sales, with approximately $52.50 million, or 44.2% of the total. Among the countries displayed, the United States has the highest sales, at approximately $25.03 million. Paseo is the top product by profit, contributing approximately $4.80 million.

The dashboard’s visuals provide context for these summary figures. The sales by country chart compares the United States, Canada, France, Germany, and Mexico. The segment chart shows how sales are divided among Government, Small Business, Enterprise, Midmarket, and Channel Partners. The product chart compares profit across Paseo, VTT, Amarilla, Velo, Montana, and Carretera. The time series shows monthly sales from September 2013 through November 2014, making it possible to see changes in sales over the displayed period.

The underlying financials data also contains fields such as units sold, COGS, gross sales, discounts, sale price, manufacturing price, product, country, segment, and date. These fields can support deeper analysis, including performance by product or market, the effect of discounts, and the relationship between sales volume and profitability. The report includes measures for Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, and Total Units Sold, as well as a Profit per Unit calculated column.
